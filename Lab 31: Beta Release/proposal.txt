Project Overview:
This project is a cellular simulation that models the transport and synthesis of 
molecules across different cellular organelles over 30 discrete time period 
(representing 30 milliseconds) to showcase cellular activity such as 
concentration gradients, membrane transport mechansims, and phtotlysis/hydrolysis 
and their affects to the biochemical balance within a cell.

Implementation:
Using std::map<std::string, std::array<std::list<std::string>, 3>> act as data structure to store and manage 
cellular data

The key (std::string) represents a specific cellular region or organelle. ex. (Nucleus, Cell Memebrane, etc.)
The value is a 3 element std::array of std::lists that represet distinct molecular categories:
 - Index 0: Ions (K+, Na+, etc.)
 - Index 1: Proteins/Enzymes (Cas9, ATP_Synthase, etc.)
 - Index 2: Metabolites (ATP, Glucose, Lipids)

Simulated Events:
The process simulated changes over 30 discrete time periods. At each time period, three events may/can occur:
 - Event 1: Passive Transport: Ions or molecules migrate between the Cell Membrane/internal organelles passively.
 - Event 2: Synthesis/Degradation: Proteins are added to the Endoplasmic Reticulum, or ATP is converted to ADP.
 - Event 3: Environmental Stress: Large and sudden influx of external ions trigger rapid transport response.
 - Event 4: Translation: Messengers in the Nucleus are used to synthesize Proteins in the Ribosome.
 - Event 5: Exocytosis: Accumulated waste is cleared out from the Lysosome.
 - Event 6: Active Transport: The cell consumes ATP to pump out Na+ and exchange it for K+.
 - Event 7: Packaging: The Golgi Apparatus prepares and packages proteins into Vesicles.
 - Event 8: DNA Replication: Genetic material is copied within the Nucleus.
 - Event 9: Apoptosis: If environmental stress is critically high (too much Na+ at the membrane), 
                       the cell self-destructs, clearing all organelles and ending the simulation.
